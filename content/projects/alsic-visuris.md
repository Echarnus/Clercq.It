Software engineer at **ALSIC**, April 2015 to July 2018.

Helped maintain and develop **VisuRIS** for De Vlaamse Waterweg, the organisation responsible for most of the Flemish fairways. VisuRIS integrates multiple RIS (river information services) applications into one, including tracking ships by their AIS transponders and showing calamities on the fairways. It is also available as an iOS and Android app.
